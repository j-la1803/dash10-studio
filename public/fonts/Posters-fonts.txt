These are the official Google Fonts sources requested by the approved
Dash 10 Posters 80 (1).html reference. CSS aliases Posters Inter and Posters Mono
scope them to the four homepage posters. Existing site fonts are unchanged.

Loading optimization:
- Posters Inter 400 reuses inter-regular-latin.woff2.
- Posters Mono 400 reuses jetbrains-mono-regular-latin.woff2.
- Their regular ASCII outlines and advance widths were verified identical to
  the reference sources using fontTools DecomposingRecordingPen.
- The two new variable files retain only U+0020, U+002E–002F, U+0030–0039,
  U+0041–005A, covering the uppercase 600/700 headlines/CTAs and 500 technical
  labels. The original variation axes, outlines and hinting are retained.
- Combined new font transfer: 17,292 bytes (before HTTP headers/compression).

Reproduce each subset from its original source with fontTools/Brotli:
pyftsubset ORIGINAL.woff2 --unicodes=U+0020,U+002E-002F,U+0030-0039,U+0041-005A --layout-features='*' --flavor=woff2 --output-file=OUTPUT.woff2
